## Worst findability problems

1. **The command palette misses most of this slice.** «Cerca e comandi» returns nothing for: automazione, assegna PE, esperienza, elimina campagna, copia campagna, fonti, predefiniti, salute dei nemici, veicoli, bastioni, riepilogo gruppo, magie condivise, cronologia and dipendenze. «archivia campagna» opens the missions «Archivio», and «importa» opens «Impostazioni» (fuzzy match). It does find: regole della tavola, impostazioni, riposo, istantanea, esporta, cestino and dati della campagna. Pattern: Foundry/Owlbear command search over every settings label. File: `command-palette.mjs` / `registry.mjs`.
2. **Settings are split and hidden behind collapsed groups.** Rules can be read under «Gioco › Regole della tavola» but edited under «Gestione DM › Impostazioni». What players see of enemies is split between the «Regole» tab (HP) and the «Predefiniti» tab (names). Every sidebar group except the active one is collapsed. «Mondo» appears twice (as an item and as a group). «Archivio», «Archivio chat» and the campaign archive in «Dati della campagna» share one word for three things.
3. **The homebrew enable path dead-ends.** «Fonti › Gestisci in Biblioteca» lands on an empty Biblioteca «Campagna» tab with no route to «Le mie creazioni › Abilita in campagna». The campaign «Homebrew» page has no add action and shows no revision number (CA-72, CA-73).
4. **Entry history is buried and mislabelled.** «Cronologia accessibile» sits collapsed at the page bottom, and it has no diff (CA-82).
5. **The running campaign cannot be archived or deleted** until another campaign is started. The typed delete confirmation needs an em dash for auto-named copies (CA-84, CA-85).
6. **At the Table, «Gruppo» means presence, not the party.** The party summary is behind «Tutti › Riepilogo del gruppo» (CA-76).
7. **Group rest is DM-only busywork.** Nobody is ticked by default, there is no «tutti», completion is per person, players get no request or hit-dice step, and the running rest hides under «Riposi registrati» after closing (CA-77).
8. **Internal census IDs leak into the UI.** Examples: «Modello: CRP-040», «D&D Beyond Game Settings (DDB-128, SVC-105)», «Roll20 «Apply Default Settings» (R20-087, SVC-101)». This is noise for a newcomer and names competitors in product copy.
9. **Smaller issues.**
   - «Nuovo voce del mondo» is a grammar error (`campaign.mjs:286`).
   - A stale undo button «Annulla: Riposo aggiornato» followed me into the Tesoro page.
   - An inferred 900-XP base appeared silently after the first award (CA-79).
