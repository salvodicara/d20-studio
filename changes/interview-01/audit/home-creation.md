## Worst findability problems

1. **The DM cannot find co-built drafts (PG-19).** A player's «Costruisci con il DM» draft does not appear in the DM's bell, Home, Personaggi or campaign. The only way in is the palette command «Bozze condivise». A newcomer DM would never learn that it exists.
2. **The DM's XP award is hidden in the Table's loot panel (PG-17).** «Assegna PE e monete» is only under Tavolo › Oggetti › Bottino, and palette searches for «PE», «punti esperienza» or «assegna» find nothing.
3. **Level-up does not open on the new level (PG-08).** «Avanza» from Home or the sheet lands on «Identità». The user must find Classi, type the new level by hand, and the new class features are not listed or added.
4. **Resuming a quick-path draft loses its choices (IN-06).** «Riprendi» on Home reopens a quick-path draft in the step-by-step builder, with the chosen class empty.
5. **«Primi passi» sits at the bottom of Home (IN-07).** It is the last of about eight blocks, and the «Una campagna o una mappa» card has no action for a player who already has a campaign.
6. **Two-step saves with the primary button disabled (IN-08, IN-04, PG-06).** In the note editor, the session form and the homebrew editor, the final save stays disabled until «Salva bozza e aggiorna anteprima» is pressed. The homebrew save also hides its extra requirement («Autore o fonte») in the preview column.
7. **The palette does not know several builder and sheet actions.** «ricostruisci», «multiclasse», «doni», «esperienza/PE», «sali di livello» and «esempio» all return 0 results, although the features exist.
8. **Silent or unclear validation in the builder (PG-03, PG-05, PG-12).** Empty background +2/+1 are marked «Completa». All six increase boxes look equally allowed. The duplicate-skill message has a blank source («concessa da :») and silently clears a species choice. «Correggi sezione» does nothing when you are already in the section.
9. **Mixed languages on the sheet.** Class and species features appear in English («Fighting Style», «Second Wind», «Darkvision», «Elven Lineage») inside the Italian UI.
10. **Dropdowns instead of cards in the step-by-step builder (PG-01, PG-06).** Class, background and species are plain dropdowns with no descriptions, while the quick path already has DDB-style cards with a detail page. A newcomer who picks «Passo per passo» gets the weaker picker.
