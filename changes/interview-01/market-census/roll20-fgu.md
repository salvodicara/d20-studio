# Market census — Roll20 and Fantasy Grounds VTT (Unity)

Access date: **23/09/2026**. Scope: every user-facing capability of the two products that is
relevant to a D&D 2024 player/DM product, mapped to the d20 Studio contract
([FEATURES](../../../docs/interview/FEATURES.md), [COVERAGE](../../../docs/interview/COVERAGE.md),
[REFERENCE_COVERAGE](../../../docs/interview/REFERENCE_COVERAGE.md), NAV ids in
[NAVIGATION](../../../docs/interview/NAVIGATION.md)). This census complements the Foundry,
Beyond and BG3/Solasta audits; it does not replace R001–R185 in RESEARCH, which remain valid
with their own dates.

## Method and access limits

- **Roll20:** the whole public Help Center was enumerated through its public Zendesk API
  (13 categories, 57 sections, 295 articles, each with its `edited_at` date); ~110 articles
  relevant to D&D play were read in full or by section, plus the 2024, 2025 and 2026 change
  logs filtered for new features. The HTML help pages return 403 to automated fetches, so the
  API body is the evidence. "ed." dates below are the article edit dates returned by the API.
  Pathfinder/Cthulhu/other-system sheets, sheet-development and billing pages were excluded.
- **Fantasy Grounds VTT (FGVTT, formerly FGU):** the official Confluence user manual (space
  FGCP, 560 pages) was enumerated through its public REST API; 75 pages relevant to CoreRPG and
  the D&D 5E ruleset were read (full or by section). Four official feature pages on
  fantasygrounds.com were read (reviewed 23/09/2026 per the site). "ed." is the page version date.
- **No account, installation, runtime test or human playtest.** Nothing here is observed
  behaviour; rows are **published capabilities** (official docs / release notes / product
  pages). Community opinion is used only as demand signal and labelled `community`.
  Absence of a row does not prove absence in the product.
- Product column: `R20 core` (Roll20 VTT/site), `R20 D&D 2024` (official "D&D by Roll20"
  Beacon sheet/builder), `R20 Mod` (third-party Mod/API script), `R20 Dungeon Scrawl`
  (Roll20-owned map maker), `FG core` (FGVTT application + CoreRPG), `FG 5E` (official D&D 5E
  ruleset), `FG Forge` (third-party extension store).
- Coverage column: an F-code/doc anchor when the contract already has the capability in
  direction; `PARTIAL` when a sub-capability or UX detail is missing; `NEW` when the contract
  has no home for it; `OUT (ref)` when an owner decision excludes it for now. These are
  pointers for later work, not decisions.
- IDs use `R20-nnn` for both products, as requested by the census template.
- Totals: **214 rows** (Roll20 132 incl. D&D 2024 sheet, Mod and map-maker rows; FGVTT 81;
  1 community). Coverage: 152 rows PARTIAL, 30 rows mainly NEW, 63 rows that name at least one
  NEW sub-capability, 6 OUT by existing owner decisions.

## Pattern notes (what to copy, why, who does it best)

1. **Preview and act as a specific player** — Roll20 Player View (2026) opens a separate tab per
   player with their exact map, chat, journal and permissions, and labels any message sent there
   as the GM acting for that player; exploration done in the preview can be reset before closing.
   It works because permission bugs are found before the session and the GM keeps their own
   view. Best: Roll20. Extends I-039 (generic player mode) — copy the per-player picker, the
   banner, the audit label and the reset window.
2. **A roll-visibility ladder, not a single toggle** — public / roller+GM / GM-only with a
   confirmation to the roller / GM-only with no trace. Add FG's dice tower (players drop dice
   the GM alone sees) and late reveal (GM middle-click or drag a hidden result to everyone or
   one player). Best: Roll20 for the ladder, FG for reveal. Conflict: Roll20 plays a sound for
   secret results that can leak information; d20 must keep physical/digital baseline and logged
   consequences.
3. **Target first, then act; resolve per target** — FG: select targets on map or tracker, then
   double-click or drag the action; each target saves, successful savers are untargeted, half
   damage/resistances/crits applied, arrows show who targets whom. Best: FG. d20 already has
   target-first in TABLE_ACTIONS; copy auto-untarget on success and visible targeting lines.
4. **Effects carry their own clock** — FG effects specify when they tick (start/end of turn, end
   of next turn, on short/long rest), whose initiative anchors them, whether players can see
   them, and whether they apply to one roll, one action or all rolls. This removes most manual
   bookkeeping of durations and hidden curses. Best: FG. Conflict: FG expresses this in a code
   syntax; d20 should expose the same fields as form choices (GAP-06), never as a mini-language.
5. **Sheet derived values are read-only with an explicit override modal** — Roll20 2024 sheet
   puts a cog on each section; totals cannot be typed over, but a modal offers "override" or
   "add bonus" per component. It prevents silent corruption and keeps corrections explicit.
   Best: Roll20 D&D 2024. Pair with the Modifier Builder's published stacking order
   (set base → add → min/max → multiply → override).
6. **Builder with resume and a review gate** — Save & exit / discard & exit / continue; a review
   step lists missing required choices; apply only when complete; level-up exit returns to the
   pre-level sheet if discarded. FG adds a commit-screen warning when mixing 2014 and 2024
   sources. Best: Roll20 (flow) + FG (compatibility warnings).
7. **Split the party across scenes with visible ribbons** — Roll20 page menu shows where each
   player is, lets the GM drag players between scenes, regroup in one click, and "place party
   here" drops all party tokens at the cursor and focus-pings cameras. Best: Roll20. d20 NAV-06/07
   already separates GM view from players' scene; copy ribbons + regroup. Conflict: forced camera
   recentring (Shift-ping/focus ping) is outside the F-051 boundary; keep it as an explicit
   choice, not a default.
8. **One party screen for group rolls, loot, order and XP** — FG Party Sheet: group save/check
   vs a DC with pass/fail list; parcel loot assigned by name and coins split evenly with
   remainder kept; sell at a set percentage; marching formation grid reused when placing tokens;
   XP award split from encounters/quests. Best: FG. Maps to F-053/F-018/F-054/F-055.
9. **Share by gesture, with per-record audience** — Roll20 per-entry "view" and "control" lists,
   "show to players" push, drag a handout onto one player's name; FG drag the record link to chat
   (everyone) or to a portrait (one player). Fast, discoverable and reversible. Best: both.
   Conflict: both products let the GM read everything; d20 keeps "Solo io" notes private (I-044–I-046).
10. **Pins anchored to a heading of a handout, with player-safe excerpts** — Roll20 map pins can
    point to a section of a handout, split player text and GM text, use a blockquote as player
    flavour text, and stay hidden until revealed; showing a pin never shares the whole handout.
    Best: Roll20 (2026). Strong fit for F-013/F-014/F-039 and GAP-05.
11. **Exploration memory the GM can edit and reset** — explored areas persist per player (Roll20
    Explorer Mode, FG fog option), can be edited or reset, and "update on drop" hides the path of
    a teleport. Best: Roll20. Conflict: Foundry V14 shares fog memory; d20 choice still open.
12. **GM-approved movement as a per-map mode** — FG token locking: players propose paths with
    waypoints (even into unseen areas), GM approves or cancels individually or all at once, and
    can undo an approval. Useful for stealth/ambush maps without banning free movement.
    Best: FG. Conflict with I-042 only if made default; propose it as an optional map mode.
13. **Audience-specific health and identity disclosure** — FG options separate ally vs enemy
    health (detailed/status/off) and token markers for GM/allies/enemies; an ID toggle shows
    "unidentified creature" or a non-ID name for NPCs, items and images. Best: FG. Maps to F-050
    and GAP-09.
14. **Turn automation switches** — skip hidden or dead actors, stop at round start, centre on the
    active token, sound for "your turn", list active effects in the turn notice. Cheap, high value,
    all opt-in. Best: FG.
15. **Contextual command bars without code** — Roll20 lets a player drag any sheet roll button to a
    bottom bar, rename/colour it, and shows character-specific token actions only while that
    token is selected; autocomplete for names. Best: Roll20. For F-064 copy the drag-to-bar and
    selection-scoped bar; skip the macro language and Mod scripts (code execution not chosen).
16. **Safe history instead of fragile undo** — page-scoped undo/redo for board edits (Roll20),
    daily rollback snapshots (Roll20) and restore-previous-save as a copy (FG), per-record
    "revert to source" for edited module content (FG), and patches that overwrite only changed
    elements (Roll20). Best: combination. Conflict: none of them undoes rules consequences
    (damage applied, resources spent); d20 must keep logged corrections separate.
17. **Per-campaign content scope with clear states** — Roll20 marks each book Available /
    Available and shared / Not available; FG sets module access Block / Allow / Auto-load; FG
    records show 2024 vs Legacy and let a campaign choose the default rules version. Best: FG for
    version clarity, Roll20 for sharing states. Licensing tiers are not copied.
18. **Guided onboarding with fewer pop-ups** — Roll20 2026 replaced what's-new pop-ups with an
    Announcements tab and gives new GMs a three-way start (upload map, premade adventure, simple
    background). FG ships a tutorial campaign and a "?" on windows that opens the matching help.
    Best: Roll20 (first run) + FG (contextual help). Maps to F-031.
19. **Labelled menus beat icon rings** — FG removed radial menus in v4.7 for labelled list
    menus, and Roll20 redesigned its right-click menu to apply to multi-selections. Evidence for
    NAV-08: every object gets a labelled context/long-press menu mirroring its inspector.
20. **Mobile scope reality check** — Roll20 delisted its native app and supports phones only for
    the character sheet/builder and rolling into a live game, not for the map; its tablet VTT is
    documented as limited. This is counter-evidence to overestimate for I-129 (full phone
    session); d20 should prototype phone map interaction deliberately rather than assume it.

### Conflicts and non-transferable patterns

- **Subscription-gated capabilities** (lighting, rollback, Mod scripts, sharing limits, storage
  quotas) are business choices; d20 has no spending authority and does not copy tiers.
- **Code as the automation surface** (Roll20 macros/Mod scripts, FG effect syntax) conflicts with
  d20's no-code direction for F-064/F-009; copy the capabilities as forms and pickers.
- **Manual turn tracker (Roll20) vs rules-aware tracker (FG)**: Roll20 deliberately keeps its
  tracker system-agnostic; FG automates 5E. d20 is single-system (2024) and should follow FG's
  depth with Roll20's simplicity of reorder, countdown items and cross-scene visibility.
- **Forced camera control** (Roll20 Shift-ping/focus ping, FG sync client view) is outside the
  F-051 boundary; treat as a separate owner-visible choice.
- **Local-first (FG) vs cloud (Roll20)**: FG's host-computer storage gives offline hosting but no
  vendor backup; Roll20 is online-only. d20 needs cloud sharing plus the F-026 offline
  consultation, so neither model transfers whole.
- **Voice/video, 3D views and in-app music** exist in these products but are excluded by owner
  decisions (F-017, no 3D environments, F-030 external music).
