# Market census — computer RPGs and tactics games (cluster CRPG)

Accessed 23/09/2026. Research input for the d20 Studio blueprint contract; **not** an owner
decision, an adoption list or proof of parity. Rows record what a product publishes or what a
community source reports; they do not import any videogame rule into the 2024 baseline.

## Scope, method and access limits

- **Products:** Baldur's Gate 3 (Larian, final Patch 8), Solasta: Crown of the Magister and its
  Dungeon Maker, Solasta II (Early Access, v0.4.x, SRD 5.2 basis), Pathfinder: Kingmaker / Wrath
  of the Righteous (Owlcat), Divinity: Original Sin 2 incl. Game Master Mode, Neverwinter Nights
  Enhanced Edition (DM client), and single patterns from XCOM 2, Into the Breach, Pillars of
  Eternity II, Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader and Crusader Kings III where the pattern transfers.
- **Reuse, not repetition:** the 18 BG3 families and 3 Solasta axes in
  [reference-audits/bg3-solasta.md](../../../docs/interview/reference-audits/bg3-solasta.md)
  (BG3-01…18, SOL-01…03) and the BG3/Solasta sources already in
  [RESEARCH](../../../docs/interview/RESEARCH.md) (e.g. R101 respec, journal docs, Winter Update)
  are referenced, not re-audited. New rows extend them with capabilities they did not list.
- **Evidence kinds:** *release notes* (official patch notes), *official dev update/docs*
  (studio blog, dev diary, official wiki/docs, staff forum post), *store page*, *community wiki*
  (bg3.wiki, Fextralife), *community guide/forum*, *press*. "Search summary" means the page itself
  could not be opened and the row relies on the search engine's summary of it: treat as unverified.
- **Blocked or partial access:** Fandom wikis (Divinity GM mode, NWN DM client) returned HTTP 402;
  the Solasta forum is closed (redirect); subsetgames.com returned 403; PC Gamer's WotR guide was
  truncated; Pathfinder turn-based UI detail beyond Owlcat's pre-release post was not found in a
  primary source. No game was installed or played; no runtime observation; no human playtest.
- **Rules boundary:** "Deviation" notes flag where the game differs from D&D 2024 as far as the
  source states it. Any rule mention still needs verification against SRD 5.2 / PHB 2024 with
  source kind and revision before it enters the contract. Karmic dice, surfaces, weight-based
  throws, +2 high ground, bonus-action shove/jump, gold-for-resurrection are **not** to be copied.
- **Coverage codes:** F-xxx = [FEATURES](../../../docs/interview/FEATURES.md); CE, TA, TM, TR =
  COMBAT_EXPLORATION, TABLE_ACTIONS, TABLE_MOVEMENT, TABLE_REACTIONS; INV, MAG, REST, ADV, CRE,
  HB, FORMS = the corresponding flow documents; NAV-xx = NAVIGATION; GAP-xx =
  [REFERENCE_COVERAGE](../../../docs/interview/REFERENCE_COVERAGE.md); BG3-xx/SOL-xx = the audit
  above. **PARTIAL** = family exists but this command/state/pattern is not yet specified;
  **NEW** = no family or anchor found.

## Pattern notes

1. **Preview before commit, everywhere.** Path length, dash ring, jump arc, shove landing, AoO
   arrows, cover and sight tiles are shown while pointing and cost nothing until confirmed.
   BG3 has the widest set (CRP-107/108/114/115); XCOM 2 has the clearest two-tier range and
   cover/flank read (CRP-113/116). Copy the preview grammar, not BG3's action costs.
2. **Reaction policy per character: enabled + Ask/Auto, then one batched prompt.** BG3's evolution
   (Patch 2 → 7) ends with a multi-character pop-up confirmed once (CRP-138/139). Solasta's timer
   (CRP-140) conflicts with tabletop pacing and with "declining does not spend": default no timeout,
   DM may set one.
3. **Every number explains itself.** Hover log lines and targets for the full breakdown, pin a
   tooltip, follow glossary links into nested tooltips (BG3 CRP-129/134; Owlcat/Paradox CRP-172).
   Best: CK3-style lockable nested tooltips for rules terms; BG3 for roll breakdowns.
4. **Separate pre-roll and post-roll windows.** BG3's "Add Bonus" before the d20 and "Roll Again"
   after failure (CRP-131/031) are clear, but each 2024 feature decides its own window (e.g. a bonus
   added after seeing the roll): the UI offers the window the feature allows, never BG3's timing.
5. **Graded knowledge instead of full stat blocks.** Solasta's 4-level bestiary unlocked by checks
   (CRP-135) and WotR's pooled knowledge (CRP-136) respect the known/unknown boundary; BG3 Examine and
   hit % (CRP-126/134) expose AC and saves. Adopt graded disclosure with DM override.
6. **One table-options panel with lock semantics and a change history.** BG3 Custom Mode, Solasta
   and WotR all bundle presets + individual switches (CRP-040…050). Copy: preset → per-switch edit;
   mark which switches lock at campaign start; log mid-campaign changes (WotR "Custom" flag). Every
   switch that alters a rule is a house rule with its own source kind.
7. **Party as a movable group.** Chain/unchain by dragging portraits, Group All, follow-leader and
   formations (CRP-123/125) map to marching order and multi-token movement; BG3 is the reference.
8. **Explicit mode switch between free exploration and turns.** BG3 Shift+Space and Owlcat's
   on-the-fly toggle (CRP-095/096) confirm the pattern F-060 already adopts.
9. **Tutorials that teach at first occurrence and are archived.** BG3 (CRP-001): pop-up the first
   time a rule applies, archive in a journal list, "Reset tutorials". Fits F-031 without a forced tour.
10. **Camp as a place and a menu.** Shared stash, send-to-camp, camp inventories (BG3 CRP-156) plus
    role assignment with checks for watch, camouflage and cooking (WotR CRP-155). Map to rest
    participation, F-055 watches and F-022 provisions; BG3 supply points are not 2024.
11. **Hotbar organised by theme and resource, with variants folded.** BG3 groups, filters, level
    variants and follow-ups in sub-menus (CRP-098/100); plus a visible resource bar (CRP-099).
12. **DM live-control kit.** Possess, spawn from a library, edit status, grant rewards, request
    rolls, show choice cards (DOS2 GM CRP-161/072; NWN CRP-163). DOS2 is the best narrative model
    (vignettes); NWN the best live-ops model. Fudging must be an explicit, logged DM ruling.
13. **Dependency check when joining.** BG3's mod verification window (CRP-171) is the right model
    for homebrew/packs a campaign requires: list missing/outdated items with one action each.
14. **Accessibility baseline from BG3 patches.** Colour-blind modes, text size and background
    opacity, hold-to-confirm for End Turn, tooltip delay, keybind categories/reset, unit system
    (CRP-180…184, 093). WotR adds animation speed (CRP-185).
15. **Trade on one screen with the whole party.** BG3: both inventories, party-wide access, wares
    marking, buyback, balance offer (CRP-159). Solasta adds restock and faction stock (CRP-160).
16. **Undo granularity.** Into the Breach separates "undo last move" from a scarce "reset turn"
    (CRP-164); BG3 relies on saves; BG3/Solasta single-save/seed options (CRP-051/049) exist to stop
    reroll abuse. d20 Studio keeps undo before dice/reveal and a logged correction after.

**Conflicts to keep explicit:** timed reactions (Solasta) vs untimed (BG3); group vote (Solasta II
CRP-053) vs single speaker with listen-in (BG3 CRP-054) — tabletop default is DM adjudication, a
poll is optional; first-to-interact loot (CRP-158) vs party stash/offers; karmic dice (CRP-049)
rejected; hit % disclosure (CRP-126) vs secrecy; BG3 shared initiative, surprise, d4 initiative,
bonus-action shove/jump, +2 high ground, weight-based throws, Help revive, gold resurrection and
level-1/2 subclasses all differ from 2024 and are recorded only as UX precedents.
