# Market census — D&D Beyond cluster

Accessed 2026-09-23. Author: research subagent for interview-01. Scope: D&D Beyond (DDB) web and
mobile app: builder, sheet, campaigns, dice and Game Log, legacy Encounters, Maps VTT, homebrew,
library and marketplace, account and subscriptions. Also covers the official Avrae bot and the
third-party Beyond20 extension and ddb-importer module where they extend DDB. This census extends
[reference-audits/beyond.md](../../../docs/interview/reference-audits/beyond.md) (families DB-01…DB-25)
down to capability and interaction level. It does not replace that audit or any decision.

## Method, access limits and evidence labels

- **Help Center.** Cloudflare blocks the HTML pages at `dndbeyond-support.wizards.com`. All 310
  public articles were read through the site's public Zendesk API
  (`/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles.json`). URLs below are each article's canonical `html_url`.
  Dates are each article's `updated_at`. Several articles say they are not yet updated for the
  new builder (Sheet Sections, Miscellaneous Features, Additional Content). Treat their details
  as possibly out of date.
- **Release notes.** Sources are the current [changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog) (entries
  from 2022 to 2026-09-21) and the [old changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/old-changelog) (2017–2022, 135 entries). The
  "Old changelog" rows are historical: they show that a pattern shipped, not that it still
  behaves the same way today.
- **Observed.** The public example sheet ([Presto](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/133058471))
  was opened without logging in, in the Browser pane (cookies: non-essential rejected). Tabs and
  sidebars could be inspected. Any edit redirected to Create Account, so no write behaviour was
  observed. No account was created, nothing was bought and no private data was opened. Maps,
  campaigns, homebrew forms and the mobile app were **not** used first-hand. Rows about them are
  published capability, not observation.
- **Community.** DDB forum threads and store reviews are demand or usage signals only. They are
  labelled `community`.
- **Announced.** Items described as coming soon (Custom Stickers or Token Art, the new mobile play
  experience, revamped search, condition effects) are labelled *announced* and are not counted
  as available.
- **Not verified.** A web-search summary claims that the 2024 Exhaustion penalties are not
  auto-applied on the DDB sheet. The primary thread could not be confirmed, so no row relies on
  it. The Exhaustion level control itself was observed (DDB-034).
- **Coverage column.** `F-0nn` means the d20 Studio family already names this capability
  (details may still be open there). `PARTIAL (F-0nn)` means the family exists but this concrete
  sub-capability or pattern is missing or only scoped. `NEW` means no family covers it. `NEW
  (commercial)` marks monetisation mechanics; a DB-09 note already refuses to adopt DDB's
  commercial limits implicitly. `EXCLUDED` marks an explicit decision already recorded (No3D).
  `OPEN F-063` marks the unresolved scope exception.
- Rules text is not reproduced. Only short factual descriptions are given.

## Pattern notes (what to copy, why, who does it best)

1. **Roll where the number is.** Clicking any number rolls it with its modifiers. Right-click
   (or long-press on touch) opens a small menu: advantage, disadvantage, flat, crit, audience
   (DDB-150, DDB-149). One gesture covers the common case, and the rare cases stay one step away
   without a dialog. *Best: DDB sheet; Beyond20 carries it into other VTTs.* Right-click and
   long-press are hidden gestures, so d20 Studio also needs a visible alternative for keyboard,
   touch and screen-reader users.
2. **Roll audience chosen inside the log.** The default audience (Everyone, Self or DM) is set in
   the Game Log header and overridden per roll (DDB-149). The privacy choice sits next to the
   history it affects. *Best: DDB.* Foundry and Roll20 put roll mode in chat instead (other
   clusters). Our F-002 already requires physical and digital dice and secret DM rolls, and this
   pattern fits those.
3. **One log, every source, attributed and expandable.** Sheet, app, VTT, tracker and Discord all
   write to one campaign log. Each entry expands to per-die detail, and shared dice make "one
   roll = one result" (DDB-148, DDB-152, DDB-195). This builds table trust and removes "did it
   count?" disputes. *Best: DDB, together with Avrae Dice Sync.*
4. **Same HP widget everywhere.** Amount plus Heal or Damage, Temp HP, Max override. It is
   identical on the sheet, in the tracker and in the token menu (DDB-032, DDB-142). One
   interaction learned serves players and DMs, and the arithmetic mistakes disappear. *Best:
   DDB.*
5. **Action-economy filters.** Actions tab chips (All, Attack, Action, Bonus, Reaction, Other,
   Limited Use) plus the generic combat actions listed inline (DDB-039). This follows the 2024
   turn structure and answers "what can I do now?" without searching. *Best: DDB sheet.*
6. **Rests as short checklists with consequences shown.** Pick Hit Dice, roll, apply as healing
   (on by default). Long Rest offers to reduce Exhaustion. Limited-use resources are grouped with
   their reset buttons (DDB-037, DDB-038). *Best: DDB.* Compare REST_TIME_FLOW for
   partial-participation needs that DDB lacks.
7. **Build the fight from the board.** "Add All Tokens" builds the encounter. Sides are grouped
   automatically, difficulty updates live with 2024 math, duplicates are lettered A–Z, hidden
   creatures never show in the player order, and late arrivals wait in a Pending lane (DDB-134,
   DDB-137–DDB-141). This cuts combat setup to a click while protecting secrets. *Best: DDB
   Maps.* Conflict: legacy DDB Encounters uses 2014 XP math, which must never be mixed in.
8. **One contextual toolbar per creature.** HP, conditions, stat block, reveal art, rename or hide
   name, colour ring, size, lock or group, add to combat, delete. It follows a stable order, has
   hotkeys and hides while dragging (DDB-117). Everything about "this creature" is one click
   away. *Best: DDB Maps; Owlbear-style minimalism is a counterweight.*
9. **Prep lives on the map, and works without one.** DM-only scene pins open their notes. Pins
   can turn semi-transparent to declutter. A "Theater of the Mind organizer" gives
   map-less and in-person tables the same prep (DDB-098–DDB-101). This removes tab-hunting
   mid-session. *Best: DDB Scene Prep.* Foundry journal pins are the richer precedent (other
   cluster).
10. **Two journals with explicit audiences.** A private DM journal and a shared party journal.
    Hotkey quick-jot. Automatic context headers. Per-entry lock while someone edits (DDB-085–087).
    Simple visibility and no overwrite conflicts. *Best: DDB (2026).* Conflict: real-time
    co-editing (Notion, LegendKeeper, Foundry) against DDB's lock. Resolve under GAP-11. Users
    also complain that journals exist only inside the VTT; d20 Studio should expose them from
    Campaign and the sheet.
11. **Reveal in one move.** Search the whole library or drop a file onto a drop zone, then
    "show". This also works from token art. A spectator window mirrors exactly what players see
    for a TV (DDB-129–DDB-132). *Best: DDB.* Watch-out: the drop zone is invisible until a drag
    starts, which confused users (DDB-131).
12. **A presentation gate with a waiting state.** Show or hide the map with one button, while
    players see a friendly waiting message and tips (DDB-111, DDB-006). The DM can switch scenes
    privately. *Best: DDB; Owlbear has an equivalent.*
13. **A small, plain permission panel.** Six or seven named switches ("players may draw / ping /
    point / move monsters / lock / group"; "show Bloodied") (DDB-128). Understandable without a
    permissions model. *Conflict:* Foundry's per-document ownership is more powerful; d20 Studio
    may need both a simple panel and per-object overrides (F-006).
14. **Builder safety nets.** A draft is created immediately. (!) marks every pending choice.
    "Sheet Ready" gating. Prerequisites are enforced with an *explicit, reversible* bypass.
    Duplicate proficiencies are freed automatically with a notice (DDB-011, DDB-012, DDB-016,
    DDB-017). This prevents invalid builds while keeping DM discretion. *Best: DDB.*
15. **Content availability as understandable toggles.** Source categories with one-line
    explanations, Legacy badges, a rule-set filter, and messages when disabled sources hide
    options (DDB-015, DDB-165). Users see *why* an option is missing. *Best: DDB.* Its
    monetisation (sharing tiers, slot locks, tokens behind Master tier) is **not** a pattern to
    copy (DB-09).
16. **Homebrew uses the same structured data as official content.** Save the base form, then add
    prerequisites, options, modifiers, spells, limited uses, level scaling and creature rules.
    Start from a template or from scratch. Published items change only through new versions, and
    users choose when to update (DDB-172–DDB-176). Homebrew behaves exactly like official content
    across builder, sheet and VTT. *Best: DDB (with Foundry as the effects precedent).* Conflicts:
    DDB's irreversible publishing and its moderation-queue versus automated-checks history.
17. **Ownership moves by claiming.** Unassigned characters, "Claim", explicit unassign. The DM can
    deactivate or remove but never seize (DDB-074, DDB-075). Ownership is always clear.
    *Conflict:* tables need the DM to reassign a character when a player quits, and DDB's
    workaround (recreate the character) is clumsy. This is a real gap for F-006.
18. **Bridges show demand for sheet-first play in other spaces.** Avrae two-way Dice Sync and
    Beyond20's sheet-to-VTT bridge (with HP push and roll policies) (DDB-195, DDB-200–DDB-202).
    Many groups keep the sheet and play elsewhere (Discord, Roll20, Foundry). This is evidence for
    GAP-17. Beyond20's user count is self-reported (2021).
19. **Mobile: last-known-good offline, plus table ergonomics.** An offline banner, the last-synced
    sheet, and an error with retry for never-opened sheets. Swipe sections with a quick-jump
    button. Keep the screen awake. A home-screen widget (DDB-060, DDB-066, DDB-068, DDB-069).
    *Best: DDB app.*
20. **Tell users what changed, in place.** A "What's New" modal in the VTT, a public changelog, a
    status page, and an entitlement-resync button with a confirmation (DDB-007, DDB-190,
    DDB-191). Fewer support loops and clearer error recovery. *Best: DDB.*

### Documented conflicts and inconsistencies inside DDB

- **Draw permission.** The Draw article says DM only (2026-02-27). The 2024-02 release says
  players can draw, and Game Settings has a player toggle.
- **Token control.** Controlling Tokens says anyone can move any token. Game Settings later adds
  a DM switch for monster tokens.
- **Quickbuilder login.** The FAQ (2026-03-24) requires login; the changelog (2026-08-24) allows
  logged-out use.
- **Free encounter cap.** The Free Tier page says 6 encounters; the Encounters article and trial
  FAQ say 8.
- **Session controls naming.** The help article (Start/Pause/End) is behind the changelog (Show/Hide
  Scene, then Display/Hide Map).
- **Homebrew publishing.** Current docs describe a moderation queue. 2018 notes describe automated
  checks with instant sharing, and beyond.md R105 keeps the historical contrast.
- **Two encounter systems.** Legacy Encounters uses 2014 math and a separate tracker. Maps uses
  2024 math. "Lift DM tools out of Maps" (announced) will refresh the Encounter Builder.

### Not found in DDB public sources (absence ≠ proof; useful for other clusters)

Real-session scheduling or RSVP (F-011). A world wiki beyond journals, calendars, factions and
quests (F-014/F-015/F-040/F-042). Walls, dynamic lighting and vision (F-005). Audio and music
(F-030, already out of scope). User macros inside DDB (only via Avrae or Beyond20; F-064). Table
safety tools (X-card, lines and veils) beyond image reporting (GAP-16). Sheet-level undo or
history. JSON export, backup or API (F-027/F-061). Campaign-wide notifications for sessions.
Homebrew full classes (explicitly unsupported).
